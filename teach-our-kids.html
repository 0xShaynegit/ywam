A way to serve at YWAM Chiang Mai

Teach our kids

Grace International School teaches the children of YWAM families and hundreds of other missionary kids, from more than twenty countries. It needs teachers. Without them, the families cannot stay.

See current vacancies What it involves

The need

A family cannot stay if there is nowhere for their children to learn.

This is the least discussed reason missionary families leave the field. Not discouragement, not funding, not danger. Schooling. A family will serve in a hard place for years and then go home because their eldest is fourteen and there is no viable option in front of them.

Grace International School exists so that families across Southeast Asia have that option in Chiang Mai. It is an established, independent school, and it is where many YWAM staff children go.

Why it matters

A teaching post here is not a support role at one remove.

The argument for this job is short, and it holds all the way along.

  1. One

    A teacher takes a class at Grace

    Third culture kids from more than twenty countries, taught properly, in a school built for exactly this situation.

  2. Two

    Which keeps a family in the field

    Their children's education stops being the reason to leave, so the family stays and keeps working.

  3. Three

    Which keeps the work going across the region

    Those families share the love of Jesus with thousands of people throughout Southeast Asia, and reach into the communities around Chiang Mai as well.

What it involves

Read this part before the job description.

Teachers at Grace who come through YWAM are YWAM staff. Like all YWAM staff, and like Grace's teachers generally, they raise their own support rather than drawing a salary. That is the single most important practical fact about this role and we would rather you met it here than three conversations in.

It is not a hidden catch, it is how the whole organisation works, and thousands of people do it. But it changes what applying looks like. You are not negotiating a package, you are building a support base, and it takes time to do properly.

What we cannot tell you from this page is what it takes in your case: a realistic monthly figure, how long support raising usually takes, whether a teaching certification is required and which, whether you would need to complete a DTS first, and who handles the visa and work permit. Ask us. Those answers exist, they are just not ours to publish unverified.

Serving at Grace

Three things you would be part of.

The kids themselves

Impacting the lives of YWAM children and many other third culture kids from more than twenty countries, at the age when someone paying attention makes the most difference.

The families behind them

Enabling YWAM families to keep sharing the love of Jesus with thousands of people throughout Southeast Asia.

The city around you

Reaching out to the people and communities in and around Chiang Mai, which is where you would actually be living, not just working.
