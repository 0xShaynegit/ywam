About us

One base, fifteen ministries, one direction

YWAM Chiang Mai is about a hundred staff working across the city and the province, in schools, children's homes, community centres, media and translation. This is who we are and where we are.

What we run Where we are

Who we are

A hundred people, and no two doing quite the same job.

The ministries of YWAM Chiang Mai are as diverse as the YWAMers who make them happen, and they have an impact locally, regionally and internationally. Home of Joy provides a home for children who have none. Create International uses arts and media to communicate the Gospel. The DTS trains disciples who go on to work across Asia. Several other schools prepare people for specialised ministry.

We are a ministry location of YWAM Thailand and part of the wider YWAM family, a movement that has been going since 1960 and now works in more than 180 nations. Everything below sits inside that.

Why here

Chiang Mai is a hub, and that is not an accident.

Because of the city's strategic location, we host a number of YWAM's international ministries rather than only our own local work. Create International's coordination office is here. So is YWAM's Business as Mission team. Chiang Mai sits within reach of a great deal of Asia that is difficult to reach from anywhere else.

In practice that means the person next to you at lunch might be working two provinces away, or two countries away, and the base functions as much as a launch point as a location.

Where we are

The old capital of the Lanna Kingdom.

Chiang Mai was established more than seven hundred years ago as the capital of the old Lanna Kingdom, and is now Thailand's second largest city. It sits seven hundred kilometres northwest of Bangkok, in the mountains of Northern Thailand, and it leads the north in business, education, religion and the arts.

Our ministries are spread across the province rather than gathered on one campus, which is why we publish a P.O. Box rather than a street address. Most locations have their own office. If you want a particular ministry, write to it directly through the contact page.
