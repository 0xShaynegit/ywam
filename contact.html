How to reach us

There is no single front desk.

Because of our many locations throughout Chiang Mai province, most ministries run their own office and their own diary. Writing to the ministry you actually want is faster than writing to us and waiting to be forwarded.

If you are not sure who that is, the base office is the right choice. So is asking us badly rather than not asking.
