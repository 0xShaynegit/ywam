Privacy Policy

Last updated July 2026. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Who we are

YWAM Chiang Mai is a base of Youth With A Mission, a global missions and discipleship movement. This policy covers the ywamchiangmai.example.com website and its program subpages, including Project Video Asia.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name and email address when you contact us, apply to a program, or submit an inquiry form. We do not use analytics cookies or third-party ad trackers on this site.

How we use it

  • To respond to inquiries and process program applications.
  • To send information you have requested about our schools, trips or staff opportunities.
  • To maintain records required for missions organization administration and safety.

How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to keep the information you share with us secure, and we do not sell or rent it to third parties. Access is limited to staff who need it to respond to your inquiry or process your application.

Sharing with third parties

We only share your information with partner ministries or program staff directly involved in the trip, school or role you have applied for, and only as needed to coordinate your participation.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete the information we hold about you at any time by contacting us using the details below.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to our office, or by post to 123 Placeholder Road, Chiang Mai 50200, Thailand.
