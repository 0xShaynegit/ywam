Donate

Your gift supports discipleship and creative missions training in Chiang Mai, and the schools, staff and outreaches that come out of it. Choose whichever method works best for you below.

Ways to give

Five ways to send a gift

PayPal

Donating by PayPal is fast, simple and secure. Note that tax-deductible receipts are not available for gifts given this way.

Give with PayPal

Credit card

Give online by credit card through YWAM Montana. You can make a one-time gift or set up a monthly recurring donation.

Ask about credit card giving

Check

Make checks payable to YWAM and mail them to our Montana office. Please include a note designating the gift to "YWAM Chiang Mai (Hub Base)".

Bank-to-bank transfer (US only)

Tax deductibility

For US residents, tax receipts are issued only for gifts made through YWAM Montana. All US contributions made this way are tax-deductible according to IRS guidelines. Gifts given directly by PayPal do not currently receive a tax receipt.
